# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners and staff of local businesses in the US (plumbers, roofers, dentists, HVAC, landscapers and similar) who received a cold email from _luma. Their situation is not uniform: each one opens the page in a different context, since the link goes in every email. They land from the email link and decide quickly whether to keep reading.

## Product Purpose
A personalized page for each recipient of an outreach email, built around a capture of their own website. Success is a call to action: finding out what the recipient is interested in (we already have their contact data) and convincing them, among all the agencies and freelancers out there, to choose _luma.

## Positioning
_luma looked at their specific website and suggests improvements that are genuinely useful for that site, not generic ones. The page proves it by showing their own site.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Each page uses the lead's own data: business name, a capture of their site, and the findings and ideas already written for their email.
- Suggestions shown must be real and useful for that specific site, never generic filler.
- English only, because recipients are in the US.
- Every page must be noindex, and its URL must not be guessable.
- The page speaks as _luma ("we"); David appears as someone on the team, not in first person.

## Brand Commitments
Voice: _luma ("we"), warm and human, written by hand. Avoid anything that looks like an AI-generated or agency template.

## Evidence on Hand
- A capture of each recipient's website (output/capturas in luma-outreach).
- Per-lead design notes and improvement ideas (diseno_razones, analisis.oportunidades in the outreach database).
- Portfolio at underluma.com/en/proyectos.
- No testimonials, client numbers or prices are available: do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Show their site first, because it is the proof that we looked.
2. Every suggestion must make sense for that site, otherwise leave it out.
3. One clear next step so the recipient tells us what interests them.
4. Never fabricate proof, numbers or prices.
